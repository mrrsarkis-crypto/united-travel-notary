About Us

A California notary public service from the team behind the United family of brands.

Who we are

United Travel Notary is a mobile notary public service based in Van Nuys, California, serving Los Angeles County. We bring commissioned notary services directly to you — your home, office, hospital, or any location — on your schedule.

Your notary

Sarkis Tulumdzhyan is a California notary public and the founder of United Travel Notary. A paralegal by profession, Sarkis has spent over a decade helping Californians navigate paperwork, deadlines, and official processes — including as the founder of United Traffic Tickets, a California document-preparation service established in 2015.

That background means every appointment is handled with the same care: verified IDs, correctly executed certificates, properly kept records, and clear communication from booking to signature.
