Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Information we collect

When you request an appointment, we collect your name, phone number, email address, service location, and details about the notarization you need. This information is used solely to schedule and perform your appointment.

Notary journal records

California law requires notaries public to maintain a journal of every notarial act, including signer names, ID details, and document types. These journal entries are kept as required by law and are not used for marketing.

How we use your information

  • To schedule, confirm, and perform your notary appointment
  • To communicate about your booking (calls, texts, emails)
  • To comply with California notary record-keeping laws

What we never do

We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing. Your ID details and document contents are treated as strictly confidential.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information. Booking requests sent by email are transmitted over standard email channels.

Your rights

California residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information (excluding records we are legally required to retain, such as notary journal entries) by emailing notary@utt-d.com.
