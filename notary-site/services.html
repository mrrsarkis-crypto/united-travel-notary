Notary Services

Every service performed at your location — home, office, hospital, or anywhere in Los Angeles County.

✍️

Acknowledgments

The most common notarial act. We verify your identity, confirm you signed willingly, and certify deeds, powers of attorney, and other documents. $15 per signature (CA statutory fee).

🤚

Jurats

For affidavits, declarations, and sworn statements. We administer the oath, witness your signature, and certify that you swore to the document's truthfulness. $15 per signature.

🗣️

Oaths & Affirmations

Verbal oaths and affirmations administered for legal proceedings, applications, and official statements — at your location or ours.

🏠

Loan Signings

Refinances, purchases, HELOCs, reverse mortgages, and seller packages. Full signing-agent handling: document review walkthrough, signatures, and same-day drop-off coordination.

🌐

Apostille Assistance

Documents destined for use abroad often need an apostille from the California Secretary of State. We prepare your documents correctly and guide the process.

🏥

Hospital & Nursing Home Visits

Bedside notarizations for patients and residents. We coordinate with facility staff and family, and work patiently with signers of limited mobility.

🌙

After-Hours Service

Evenings, weekends, and urgent same-day appointments. Documents don't keep office hours — neither do we. After-hours surcharge quoted upfront.

📋

Copy Certifications

Certified copies of powers of attorney and other eligible documents, per California notary law.

✦ What to expect

Every Visit Includes

✓

ID Verification

Valid, unexpired government photo ID checked for every signer, per California law.

✓

Proper Execution

Signatures witnessed, oaths administered, and the official seal applied correctly.

✓

Journal & Records

Every act recorded in the official notary journal as required by the state.

A California notary public may not give legal advice, explain document contents beyond the notarial certificate, or prepare legal documents. For legal questions, consult a licensed attorney.
